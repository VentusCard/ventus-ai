How the pieces fit

Growth Plays, Skills, and the decision-outcome graph

Skills

Reusable capabilities: enrichment, financial-state detection, eligibility checks, policy controls, workflow payload creation, and measurement.

Growth Plays

Business strategies assembled from those skills. Example: qualify an advisor handoff when there is evidence of active liquidity and planning intent.

Decision-outcome graph

The shared learning layer that connects signals, actions, channels, policies, and outcomes so Ventus learns what creates incremental value.

Why this matters to a bank

We start with each team's own P&L, then earn the right to connect more.

Initial service-line use cases

  • Consumer Banking: protect primary deposits and identify relationship erosion early.
  • Merrill: identify qualified liquidity, active planning demand, and advisor-ready wealth opportunities.
  • Later, with authorization: test whether cross-business signals create incremental lift beyond each team's standalone data.

Ventus's scalable contribution

  • Turn fragmented activity into a governed decision.
  • Put the decision where employees already work.
  • Prove whether the decision created incremental value.
  • Configure data, policies, products, and channels per institution without rebuilding the core.
How to present the POC

The demo should let the product do the talking.

For the CEO / business sponsor

Open on the P&L objective. Show one qualified moment, one employee action, and the measurement design that determines whether to scale.

For the CTO / operating partner

Show the same run as a controlled system: source receipt, enrichment and decision, policy checks, Salesforce delivery receipt, and durable audit path.

Recommended demonstration sequence: Prove -> Experience -> Activate -> Measure. Technical receipts and detailed governance remain available on demand, rather than taking over the main story.

The next 30 days

What turns the POC into a credible bank pilot

1. Make the live path dependable

Deploy the connector stack and configure server-side secrets so the public demo can run Plaid and Salesforce live.

2. Make evidence durable

Move the evidence ledger to Postgres and retain a hash-verifiable decision-to-delivery trail.

3. Prove business value

Run one sanctioned Growth Play with bank outcome data, a holdout, and jointly agreed success gates.

The product is not an AI dashboard. It is a repeatable operating loop: find the right moment, prepare the right action, deliver it into work, and learn whether it changed the bank's economics.
