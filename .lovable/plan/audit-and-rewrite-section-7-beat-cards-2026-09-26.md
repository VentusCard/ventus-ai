# Audit and Rewrite Section 7 Beat Cards

## Audit findings
- Section 7’s purpose is long-term relationship growth: use a fuller customer understanding to identify the next relevant bank product, deepen the primary relationship, and support the stated 5.5% LTV lift.
- The current beat cards—“Detect the life moment,” “Prepare the next conversation,” and “Coordinate thoughtful outreach”—describe an advisor-outreach workflow that is not shown in the phone.
- The phone instead presents three personalized products: a preferred mortgage, a premium rewards card, and guided investing.
- Unlike sections 5 and 6, section 7’s cards are still single-line labels and do not explain the progression visible in the experience.

## Rewrite
Convert the three section 7 cards to the same two-line structure used in sections 5 and 6:

1. **Anticipate the next life moment**  
   A home-buying signal becomes a relevant relationship-rate mortgage recommendation.

2. **Turn routines into relationship value**  
   Ricky’s tennis routine becomes a rewards card aligned to how he spends.

3. **Bring more of the relationship home**  
   Outside brokerage transfers become a timely guided-investing opportunity.

## Scope
- Change only section 7’s three beat-card descriptions.
- Keep the section heading, subtitle, value block, phone content, three product cards, continuous rotation, navigation, and visual styling unchanged.
- Preserve the existing two-line beat-card presentation shared with sections 5 and 6.
