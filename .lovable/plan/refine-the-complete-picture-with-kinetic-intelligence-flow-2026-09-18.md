# Refine “The Complete Picture” with kinetic intelligence flow

## Goal
Make this `/deckmo` slide feel more spacious, alive, and executive-ready while preserving its complete-picture story and current presentation chrome.

## Design direction
- Use the selected **Kinetic Intelligence Flow** composition.
- Keep the strict white presentation canvas with deep navy type, blue internal signals, and gold external signals.
- Preserve the current deck typography system: bold modern headings and clean Manrope body copy, without changing other slides.
- Increase the separation between the title block and the diagram, and give the center customer more visual dominance.
- Lighten the two source panels with more internal spacing, softer borders, restrained shadows, and less visual density.
- Keep every existing label and pill, including **SKU-level purchase data**.

## Motion
- Add two slow, offset breathing rings around the centered customer.
- Send small blue and gold signal particles inward along the connecting lines.
- Use a gentle customer-node glow at the peak of each pulse cycle.
- Keep cycles slow with quiet pauses so the slide remains suitable for a bank presentation.
- Disable nonessential movement when reduced motion is enabled.
- Preserve the existing two-step reveal: the resting composition appears first; the active intelligence flow strengthens on the next step.

## Scope
- Update only the “Complete Picture” scene and its local animation styles.
- Do not change `/bankdemo`, deck copy, slide order, header, footer, presenter controls, or other beats.

## Validation
- Check both reveal states and keyboard/footer navigation.
- Verify clean framing at 1024×768, 1440×900, and 1920×1080.
- Confirm no clipping, horizontal overflow, external requests, runtime errors, or build errors.
