# Simplify the Homepage Hero Animation

## Goal
Make the hero immediately understandable and visually calm while preserving the signal-first story.

## Changes
- Keep the existing headline, supporting copy, two calls to action, Manrope type, floating navigation, and light blue background.
- Replace the detailed diagram with three large, simple elements:
  1. **Signals** — two compact labels for Internal Signals and External Intelligence.
  2. **Ventus Intelligence** — one central blue core with a subtle pulse.
  3. **Personalized Outcomes** — one compact destination block on the right.
- Animate a small number of soft light particles from left to center to right in a continuous loop.
- Remove the five individual signal-family rows, example signal text, audience labels, status badges, branching paths, stage rail, and scroll-controlled reveal states.
- Shorten the hero to one viewport so visitors can scroll naturally into The Gap without completing a long sequence.
- Keep a reduced-motion version that displays the complete flow without movement.

## Validation
- Check the full hero at desktop and mobile widths.
- Confirm the animation is legible at a glance, does not overlap the headline or navigation, and releases naturally into the next section.
- Confirm the page builds cleanly with no browser errors.
