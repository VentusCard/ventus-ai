# Audit Deckmo’s section headings and descriptions

## Outcome
Produce an audit and recommendations for review. Do not change the deck’s copy, design, or behavior.

## Scope
Review all ten sections: Thesis, The Visibility Gap, The Complete Picture, Meet Ricky, Immediate: Lower Service Cost, Mid-Term: Profit, Mid-Term: Growth, Long-Term: Retention & NPS, Tools for the Bank, and The Ask.

Include:
- Section names in the presentation header and table of contents.
- Visible eyebrows, headlines, and supporting descriptions.
- Step-specific captions and descriptions, including the retention showcase’s separate header.
- Supporting panel headings where they affect the section’s message.

Phone conversations, individual signal details, and the full embedded platform’s copy are outside this audit, except when needed to check whether a heading accurately describes what is shown.

## Review criteria
- **Alignment:** Does the copy describe the actual slide and its reveal?
- **Narrative:** Does each section advance the story from visibility to understanding, customer value, bank-wide use, and the close?
- **Clarity:** Flag jargon, vague language, repetition, grammatical issues, and unnecessarily long descriptions.
- **Consistency:** Check capitalization, terminology, time horizons, and bank/customer versus credit-union/member wording.
- **Credibility:** Flag absolute promises or outcome claims that need qualification or evidence; do not invent supporting figures.
- **Tone:** Keep recommendations executive-readable and customer-first, without surveillance-like language or public infrastructure references.

## Deliverable
A section-by-section table with:
1. Current rendered heading and description, with slide number.
2. Verdict: keep, minor polish, or rethink.
3. Specific issue and rationale.
4. Suggested replacement only where a change is warranted.

Finish with a short prioritized list of the most important changes and any recurring issues across the deck. Clearly distinguish recommendations from approved copy.

## Technical verification
Trace the script fields through the active scene renderers so unused or superseded strings are not presented as current slide copy. Check the bank and credit-union wording paths, and compare headings with the relevant slide content and internal steps. Any runtime-only behavior not verified will be marked as unverified.

## Boundaries
No source edits, layout changes, navigation changes, animation changes, new metrics, or publishing. A rewrite would be a separate decision after reviewing the audit.
