# Redesign “The Complete Picture” around five signal families

## Goal
Turn the slide from a source-only diagram into the deck’s clearest explanation of the Ventus intelligence model: internal and external inputs combine into one living customer view, which resolves into five distinct, governed signal families.

## Design
- Keep the existing title, deck header/footer, white executive presentation style, and centered customer as the visual anchor.
- Replace the two large source cards with compact **Inside the Walls** and **Outside the Walls** feeder rails at the upper left and upper right. Preserve the existing source examples, including **SKU-level purchase data**.
- Use the lower and middle canvas for a large five-family “intelligence spectrum” connected to the customer:
  - **Spending Habits** — category and merchant-theme patterns
  - **Life Events** — corroborated transitions such as relocation, college preparation, and home purchase
  - **Financial Signals** — loans, leases, mortgages, and investments
  - **Demographic** — household composition and stage
  - **Risk** — servicing and wellness only; not offer targeting
- Give each family a large, readable color-coded panel using the established deck mapping: blue, amber, emerald, violet, and rose. Keep backgrounds soft, borders darker, and labels legible from a boardroom screen.
- Make the family panels feel like five lenses on the same person rather than five disconnected settings cards. Use clean connector paths and a shared baseline to reinforce one unified profile.

## Story beats and motion
1. **Beat one:** internal and external source rails feed the breathing customer node; the five-family area remains quiet and lightly outlined.
2. **Beat two:** the five families illuminate sequentially from left to right, their connectors resolve, and the central label changes emphasis to the unified customer view.
- Keep movement restrained, presentation-safe, and disabled under reduced-motion preferences.

## Copy and scope
- Pull the family names and policy meanings from the current `/bankdemo` System tab.
- Keep descriptions short and executive-readable; do not reproduce settings controls, toggles, counts, or operational UI.
- Preserve all other deck slides and navigation unchanged.

## Validation
- Verify both beats at 1024×768, 1540×855, and 1920×1080.
- Confirm no clipping or overlap, readable family labels, correct deck navigation, reduced-motion behavior, and a clean preview build.
