# Ventus AI product goal

> **🧭 VISION / TARGET STATE (as of 2026-08).** This is the product north-star, not a description of shipped functionality. Many capabilities described here are planned, not built.

Design Ventus AI as an enterprise decision and activation platform that helps each
financial-services business turn its available transaction, customer, product, behavioral,
and external data into timely, governed, measurable growth actions.

## Product requirements

- Demonstrate the complete journey from raw data ingestion through enrichment, financial-state
  detection, recommendation, workflow activation, and measured outcome.
- Create standalone value within each business line using data, policies, products, and workflows
  that the team already controls.
- Deliver user-facing value to bankers, advisors, operators, and executives inside their existing
  systems rather than requiring another passive dashboard.
- Prioritize material P&L outcomes, initially deposit primacy and retention for Consumer Banking,
  and qualified relationship growth and net new assets for Wealth Management.
- Make Ventus's contribution explicit: transform raw behavior into decision-ready evidence,
  identify an actionable moment, select an intervention, apply policy, activate an existing
  workflow, and learn from the result.
- Use AI to create decision capabilities that teams do not have today, not merely summarize data
  or automate an existing task.
- Require every Growth Play to work independently within one business line. Another team's data
  or participation cannot be a prerequisite for its first proof of value.
- Treat governed cross-business intelligence as an expansion capability. When authorized, it may
  improve timing, qualification, and customer continuity while preserving each team's ownership,
  permissions, policy boundaries, and economic objectives.
- Measure the incremental value of connected data instead of assuming that more data is better.
- Keep the platform institution-neutral while configuring mappings, taxonomies, policies,
  products, eligibility rules, channels, and workflows for each institution and business line.
- Separate demonstrated, simulated, and future capabilities. Never claim production accuracy,
  integration, security, certification, or economic impact without matching evidence.
- Make every Growth Play a measurable operating loop with source evidence, confidence, rationale,
  policy, destination, ownership, holdout assignment, delivery receipt, and outcome tracking.
- Optimize for employee adoption, executive economics, explainability, security, compliance,
  enterprise integration, and minimal disruption to current operations.
- Build a compounding decision-and-outcome graph that learns which signals, actions, timing,
  channels, and policies generate incremental value by cohort, institution, and service line.

## MVP standard

A single business line can provide one sanctioned dataset, watch Ventus identify a qualified
customer moment, understand why an action is appropriate, deliver it into an existing employee
workflow, and measure incremental value against a holdout without depending on another team's data.

## Expansion standard

After independent value is demonstrated, authorized signals can support coordinated opportunities
across business lines while preserving data entitlements, customer ownership, policy controls, and
separate measurement. Ventus must quantify the additional lift created by the connection beyond
the standalone results.

**Strategic sequence:** team-level value → measurable operating loop → governed connection →
institution-wide learning advantage.
