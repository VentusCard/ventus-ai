# FinovateFall Mainstage — 7-Minute Script

**Demo:** Ventus AI `/bankdemo`
**Runtime:** 7:00 · ~950 spoken words at ~140 wpm
**Format:** live demo, narrated. Stage directions in `[brackets]`.

Before you walk on: `/bankdemo` already open on the **Systems** tab, Ricky pre-warmed on a Banking Personalization tab in a second browser tab, sidebar collapsed to full height, screen at 1440px or wider.

---

## 0:00 - 0:45 — Cold open

`[Systems tab on screen, no clicking yet]`

Every bank in this room is sitting on the most intimate dataset in a person's life. You know where they eat, where they travel, when their kid started looking at colleges, when their car loan is about to come due.

And then you send them a credit card offer addressed to "Valued Customer."

That gap — between what the bank *knows* and what the customer actually *receives* — is the entire opportunity. We built Ventus AI to close it. Not with another dashboard. With an intelligence layer that turns raw transactions into decisions your bank can act on, in every channel, the same day.

Let me show you the whole loop in six minutes.

**Landing sentence:** *The data is already yours. The intelligence isn't — yet.*

---

## 0:45 - 1:45 — Systems

`[stay on Systems, let the signal ticker roll once]`

This is the intelligence core. Raw card and account activity goes in on the left. What comes out is not a category code — it's a signal, with the evidence that produced it.

Five families. **Behavioral** — how someone actually lives: active living, travel, dining, pets, family, home. **Life Event** — the moments that change a financial life: a home purchase, a new baby, college prep for a dependent, a relocation. **Financial** — the balance-sheet reality: payroll cadence, deposits trending, credit headroom, funds walking out the door to an outside brokerage. **Demographic** — inferred, not asked. And **Risk**, watched quietly in the background.

`[point to an external signal row]`

And we go beyond the bank's four walls. External intelligence adds things the ledger can't see — like an auto loan at another lender approaching maturity.

**Landing sentence:** *Everything downstream is built from these five families — and every signal carries its receipt.*

---

## 1:45 - 2:45 — Intelligence Database

`[click Intelligence Database]`

Once every customer is described in signals, the bank's own book becomes something you can ask questions of. In plain language.

`[run or open a saved question]`

Not "pull me a report next quarter." Ask it now. Which households are showing college-prep behavior. Who's leaking deposits to an outside brokerage. Which segment is quietly growing.

`[open the evidence behind a result]`

And this is the part that gets you through a model risk review: every number here opens up. You can see the cohort, the signals that qualified them, and the behavior underneath. No black box on a slide — an answer with its evidence attached.

**Landing sentence:** *Your book of business, finally queryable in the language your business people speak.*

---

## 2:45 - 3:45 — AI Coworker

`[click AI Coworker, land on the live work stream]`

Now insight has to become work. This is the AI Coworker — and this is what it's doing right now, across the bank, continuously.

`[let two or three items roll past, then open one email brief]`

It doesn't hand a banker a spreadsheet. It hands them a brief.

Here's the customer moment. Here's the evidence that triggered it — the actual behavior, not a score. Here's the recommended action, a specific product for a specific reason. And here's the confidence, honestly stated.

`[gesture at the persona settings if visible]`

And the bank stays in control. Every coworker runs a playbook you define: what it always does, what it sometimes does, and what it never does. It serves every team, around the clock, and it never goes off script.

**Landing sentence:** *A banker starts their morning with decisions, not data pulls.*

---

## 3:45 - 4:45 — Automated Flows

`[click Automated Flows]`

One banker at a time doesn't move a bank. So here's the same intelligence at portfolio scale.

`[scroll the catalog briefly]`

Seventy-plus product plays — lending, deposits, cards, wealth, insurance, small business — and each one is assembled from signals, not from a static segment somebody defined two years ago.

`[open one flow, toggle a signal off, then back on]`

Watch this. Turn a signal off — the audience recalculates. Turn it back on — it comes right back. Marketing can compose an audience the way a builder composes a query, see the reach before they commit, and launch it. No SQL, no ticket, no six-week wait.

**Landing sentence:** *Segments used to be built once a year. Now they're built out of live behavior.*

---

## 4:45 - 6:15 — Banking Personalization (three surfaces)

`[click Personalized Deals — empty state on screen]`

Last piece. This is what the customer actually feels — and until we pick someone, it's deliberately blank. No customer, no personalization.

`[search and select Ricky]`

Ricky. On the left, his signals — the same five families you saw in the core. On the right, generating live from those signals right now.

`[let the phone surface populate]`

**Deals.** Not the bank's inventory of offers. Offers built out of how Ricky actually lives — and notice the language. It's specific enough that he recognizes himself in it, and never so specific that it feels like surveillance.

`[click Personalized Product]`

**Product.** Same signals, different job. His life events and financial position surface the next product that makes sense for him — with the reason he'd accept it sitting right there.

`[click Personalized Relationship]`

**Relationship.** And on the tablet, the full banking relationship — a conversation with a bank that clearly remembers who he is.

One customer. One set of signals. Three surfaces that all agree with each other — because they're drawing from the same intelligence.

**Landing sentence:** *Three channels, one truth about the customer.*

---

## 6:15 - 7:00 — Close

`[step away from the screen]`

That's the loop. Transactions in. Five families of signals out. Queryable intelligence for the analyst, briefed work for the banker, composable flows for marketing, and a personalized experience for the customer — all from one layer.

The banks that win the next decade won't be the ones with the most data. Every bank has the data. They'll be the ones who can act on it before the moment passes — while the customer is still looking at colleges, while that loan is still coming due, while they still have the chance to be the obvious answer.

That's Ventus AI. Thank you.

Use these if a live generation is slow or a click doesn't land. Keep talking, keep moving.

- **Systems:** "Five signal families — behavioral, life event, financial, demographic, risk — plus outside-the-bank intelligence. Every signal carries the evidence that produced it."
- **Intelligence Database:** "Ask your book of business a question in plain English, get an answer with the cohort and evidence attached."
- **AI Coworker:** "Bankers get a brief, not a spreadsheet: the moment, the evidence, the recommended action, the confidence — inside a playbook the bank controls."
- **Automated Flows:** "Seventy-plus product plays, each composed from live signals, with the audience recalculating as you toggle."
- **Personalization:** "Pick a customer, and deals, product and relationship all generate from the same signals — specific enough to recognize yourself, never creepy."

**If a personalization surface hasn't rendered:** don't wait on it. Talk to the left panel. "The signals are the input — this is what the surface is being built from," then move to the next tab and come back.

2. The persona-playbook mention in AI Coworker (~12s)
3. The signal-toggle interaction in Automated Flows — narrate it instead of clicking (~15s)

5. Half the cold open — start at "Every bank in this room is sitting on…" and cut to the Systems beat (~20s)

Never cut: the Systems five-family framing, and the Ricky selection moment. Those two carry the whole story.
